China’s PhD Glut Drives Top Graduates to High School Teaching Jobs

Caixin Global | FULLTEXT | 611 words | 2026-09-17 08:25

1. A growing number of doctoral graduates from elite global and domestic universities are bypassing traditional careers in academia to become secondary school teachers in China's major cities, driven by an oversupplied labor market and fierce competition for university posts.
