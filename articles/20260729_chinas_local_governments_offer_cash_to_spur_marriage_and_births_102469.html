China’s Local Governments Offer Cash to Spur Marriage and Births

Caixin Global | FULLTEXT | 392 words | 2026-07-29 11:25

1. Chinese local governments, villages, and companies are offering cash payments and consumption vouchers to newly married couples to address persistently low marriage and birth rates. These incentives, generally for first-time marriages of locally registered residents, typically range from 1,000 to 3,000 yuan, with some villages offering much more and additional childbirth subsidies.
