Xi Opens U.S. State Visit as Trump Gives Rare Airport Welcome

Caixin Global | FULLTEXT | 600 words | 2026-09-24 02:56

1. Chinese President Xi Jinping arrived in Washington on Wednesday for a state visit at the invitation of U.S. President Donald Trump, receiving an unusual personal airport welcome from Trump and First Lady Melania Trump at Joint Base Andrews in suburban Maryland shortly after the plane landed at 5:30 p.m. The airport reception by a sitting president is a rare protocol gesture for a foreign leader

In a written statement released by China's official Xinhua News Agency, Xi said China and the U.S. should be "partners rather than rivals" and called for a more stable relationship between the world's two largest economies. Xi recalled that the two leaders had agreed during Trump's state visit to China in May to build a "constructive, strategically stable" bilateral relationship, and he urged both sides to work in the same direction toward a relationship featuring "cooperation as the mainstay, moderate competition, manageable differences, and promises of peace"

4. The airport ceremony unfolded in detailed sequence: Xi's aircraft landed at about 5:36 p.m., and U.S. personnel rolled out a red carpet as officials and an honor guard lined up. At about 5:58 p.m., Trump's presidential limousine, "The Beast," arrived, and Trump and Melania walked toward the aircraft stairs. Before Xi emerged, Trump spoke briefly with Chinese Ambassador Xie Feng and Assistant Foreign Minister Hong Lei, who then signaled the aircraft
