In Depth: China Bets Green Hydrogen’s Future on Industrial Demand

Caixin Global | FULLTEXT | 656 words | 2026-10-27 08:41

1. China is intensifying efforts to make green hydrogen commercially viable, offering billions of yuan in incentives to replace fossil-fuel-derived hydrogen in steelmaking, chemical production, and other heavy industries. The core challenge is cutting production costs enough to persuade industrial users to make the switch.

In mid-August, China's Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, Ministry of Finance, and National Development and Reform Commission jointly unveiled a plan designating five cross-provincial city cluster pilot zones for hydrogen applications, with each eligible for up to 1.6 billion yuan (about $239.1 million) in central government incentives over four years.

Beijing's 15th Five-Year Plan blueprint (2026–2030) targets cutting the average end-user price of hydrogen from roughly 30 yuan per kilogram to below 25 yuan by 2030, with a goal of around 15 yuan in resource-rich regions.

Meanwhile, a national energy plan released in June incorporated hydrogen and other green fuels into the country's non-fossil energy system for the first time, setting a goal of producing 2 million tons of green hydrogen annually by 2030 — eight times the 250,000 tons recorded in 2025.

3. China's hydrogen strategy has long centered on transportation, particularly fuel cell vehicles, but advances in battery technology have made electric heavy-duty trucks more competitive, narrowing the vehicle market.
