China Banks Report Rise in Nonperforming Retail Loans

Caixin Global | FULLTEXT | 469 words | 2026-09-03 02:44

Bank of Communications Co. Ltd.

Bank of Communications Co. Ltd., one of China’s major state-owned banks, saw the sharpest deterioration in retail asset quality among the six large lenders. Its personal nonperforming loan ratio rose 0.44 percentage point to 2.02% by end-June 2026, driven by stricter risk classification and weaker household repayment capacity.

Industrial and Commercial Bank of China Ltd.

Industrial and Commercial Bank of China Ltd. reported a rise in its retail nonperforming loan ratio by 0.19 percentage point to 1.77% by end-June 2026, according to the article. This was among the six major state-owned banks that all posted higher personal bad-loan ratios.

Ping An Bank Co. Ltd.

According to the article, among 10 listed joint-stock banks, Ping An Bank Co. Ltd. was the only exception, with its retail nonperforming loan ratio unchanged at 1.23% in the first half of 2026, while nine others reported increases.

China Bohai Bank Co. Ltd.

China Bohai Bank Co. Ltd. reported a 1.13-percentage-point rise in its retail nonperforming loan ratio to 4.93% by end-June 2026, among listed joint-stock banks showing deteriorating retail asset quality.

Huaxia Bank Co. Ltd.

Huaxia Bank Co. Ltd. reported a rise in its personal nonperforming loan ratio by 0.74 percentage point to 2.85% in the first half of 2026, reflecting deteriorating retail asset quality amid stricter risk classification and weaker household repayment capacity.

Shanghai Rural Commercial Bank Co. Ltd.

According to the article, Shanghai Rural Commercial Bank Co. Ltd. bucked the trend of deteriorating retail asset quality, improving its personal nonperforming loan ratio to 1.55% in the first half of 2026.

China Merchants Bank Co. Ltd.

China Merchants Bank Co. Ltd. is a joint-stock bank. Its chief risk officer, Xu Mingjie, said retail asset quality is the main factor affecting overall asset quality, with risks from consumer co-debt still emerging. The bank faces pressure from rising nonperforming retail loans and higher impairment provisions.

Everbright Securities Co. Ltd.

According to the article, Everbright Securities Co. Ltd. (光大证券股份有限公司) is mentioned via its chief financial industry analyst, Wang Yifeng, who noted that listed banks increased impairment provisions by 17.8% year-on-year in the first half of 2026.

China Everbright Bank Co. Ltd.

According to the article, China Everbright Bank Co. Ltd. listed a credit card bad asset portfolio in early August with outstanding principal and interest of 10.8 billion yuan ($1.6 billion), the largest such offering so far this year.

Shanghai Pudong Development Bank Co. Ltd.

Shanghai Pudong Development Bank Co. Ltd. listed a 7.2-billion-yuan nonperforming loan portfolio in July, consisting largely of higher-end customers, including platinum cardholders, according to Caixin.

Western Securities Co. Ltd.

Western Securities Co. Ltd. reported that bulk transfers of personal business loan principal and interest totaled 10.7 billion yuan in the first half of 2026, up 46.3% from a year earlier, as a sluggish macroeconomy strained small business operators' cash flow and weakened repayment capacity.
