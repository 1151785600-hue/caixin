Profile: Liao Min’s Path From Banking Regulator to IMF Nominee

1. Liao Min, a former economics student and campus folk singer at Peking University, built a career spanning commercial banking, financial regulation, economic policymaking, and international negotiations with U.S. officials and creditors of developing countries.

2. Born in December 1968, Liao earned bachelor’s and master’s degrees from Peking University’s School of Economics and an MBA from Cambridge University’s Judge Business School in 2006, where he made the dean’s list.

4. As trade tensions escalated, Liao became a core member of China’s negotiating team, participating in multiple rounds of high-level consultations between 2018 and the January 2020 phase one trade agreement, including leading a delegation to Washington in September 2019.
