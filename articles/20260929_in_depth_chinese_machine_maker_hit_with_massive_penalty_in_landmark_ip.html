In Depth: Chinese Machine Maker Hit With Massive Penalty in Landmark IP Theft Case

Caixin Global | FULLTEXT | 307 words | 2026-09-29 03:58

Beijing Jingdiao Group Co.

Beijing Jingdiao Group Co. develops and manufactures precision CNC machine tools. It won a landmark trade-secrets case, securing ¥379.63 million in damages plus ¥2 million in costs, after a former employee stole 37,340 design drawings covering 27 series and 160 machine models.

Shenzhen Create Century Machinery Co. Ltd.

Shenzhen Create Century Machinery Co. Ltd. is a subsidiary of Guangdong Create Century Intelligent Equipment Group, operating in the CNC-machine-tool sector. The Supreme People's Court ordered it and former employee Tian Xianyin to pay 381.63 million yuan for stealing trade secrets from Beijing Jingdiao Group. Authorities seized 76.01 million yuan from its accounts and froze stakes in two subsidiaries.

Guangdong Create Century Intelligent Equipment Group

Guangdong Create Century Intelligent Equipment Group is a listed machine-tool maker (300083.SZ) operating in the CNC-machine-tool sector via its Shenzhen subsidiary. The subsidiary was ordered to pay ¥381.63 million for trade-secret infringement, reducing 2025 net profit by about ¥278 million and triggering a ~30% market-capitalization drop.

Create Century Group

Create Century Group (300083.SZ) operates in the CNC-machine-tool sector via Shenzhen Create Century Machinery. In a December 2025 court ruling, it and a former employee were ordered to pay 381.63 million yuan for trade-secret theft. The case cut its 2025 net profit by about 278 million yuan, and its market cap fell roughly 30%.

Geely Holding Group

According to the article, Geely Holding Group holds the record for the largest technical-trade-secret case handled by China's IP tribunal. In 2024, it secured a judgment exceeding 640 million yuan against electric-vehicle maker WM Motor, making its case the top award in that category.

WM Motor

The article mentions WM Motor as an electric-vehicle maker that lost a trade secret dispute to Geely Holding Group in 2024, with a judgment exceeding 640 million yuan—the largest such case handled by China’s IP tribunal at the time.
