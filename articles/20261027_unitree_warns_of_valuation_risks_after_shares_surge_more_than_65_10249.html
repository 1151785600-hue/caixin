Unitree Warns of Valuation Risks After Shares Surge More Than 65%

Caixin Global | FULLTEXT | 382 words | 2026-10-27 03:30

Unitree Robotics

Unitree Robotics, a Chinese humanoid robot maker listed on Shanghai's STAR Market, saw shares surge over 65% after a J.P. Morgan double upgrade, but warned investors of valuation risks due to a high P/E ratio (465.2) and early-stage commercialization challenges. Founder Wang Xingxing noted production of tens of thousands of robots annually, with potential industry demand reaching 10 million units.

J.P. Morgan

Based on the article, J.P. Morgan upgraded Unitree Robotics two notches (from "underweight" to "overweight") on Oct. 25, raising its December 2027 price target to 995 yuan. The upgrade followed the World Physical AI Conference. However, J.P. Morgan Securities (China) also recorded institutional selling during a limit-up session on Oct. 23, indicating some profit-taking alongside the positive outlook.

China International Capital Corp.

According to the article, China International Capital Corp. (中金公司) was associated with one of the major selling seats during Unitree Robotics' recent stock surge. It was among the five largest selling seats that sold 816 million yuan worth of shares on Monday, contributing to the selling pressure amid the company's rally.

Goldman Sachs

According to the article, Goldman Sachs was associated with major selling seats during the trading of Unitree Robotics shares. Specifically, alongside China International Capital Corp. and GF Securities, Goldman Sachs was listed among the major selling seats during the recent stock surge, while institutional purchases were also recorded.

GF Securities

According to the article, GF Securities was associated with one of the major selling seats during Unitree Robotics' stock rally. Along with China International Capital Corp. and Goldman Sachs, GF Securities was listed among the top sellers, contributing to substantial selling pressure despite institutional purchases.

J.P. Morgan Securities (China)

According to the article, J.P. Morgan Securities (China) was an institutional seller of Unitree Robotics shares during the Oct. 23 limit-up session, with selling recorded through one of its trading seats. Separately, the parent firm J.P. Morgan upgraded Unitree to "overweight" on Oct. 25, but that action is attributed to the broader institution.

DeepSeek

DeepSeek is an AI startup mentioned as a Unitree Robotics shareholder. It committed to a 36-month lock-up period for its pre-IPO holdings, longer than the typical 12-month lock-up for most strategic investors, which could pressure Unitree's stock in August 2027 if earnings fall short.
