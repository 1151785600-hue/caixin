Zhilian, a dating app launched in late 2023, was exposed as an organized scam using a "chat-to-earn" model. Female operators, recruited and trained with fake profiles and stories, built fictitious emotional ties with male users to induce them into buying virtual gifts. Court rulings in April 2026 confirmed the fraud, revealing it involved over 34 million yuan and more than 7,000 victims, with over 100 defendants sentenced.
