Seres, Huawei Come to New Agreement Over Aito

Caixin Global | FULLTEXT | 310 words | 2026-10-09 10:07

Seres Group Co. Ltd.

Seres Group Co. Ltd. is a Chongqing-based automaker and Huawei's first partner under its Harmony Intelligent Mobility Alliance, launching the Aito EV brand in 2021. In September, it took full control of Aito. It recently signed a new five-year cooperation deal with Huawei, amid local government pressure to boost smart EV production.

Huawei Technologies Co. Ltd.

Huawei Technologies Co. Ltd., a tech giant, recently signed a new five-year strategic cooperation with Seres for the Aito EV brand. It previously held dominance over Aito’s design and sales but ceded full control in September, now focusing resources on other HIMA brands like Luxeed, Shangjie, Maextro, and Stelato.

Chery Automobile Co. Ltd.

Chery Automobile Co. Ltd. is a Chinese automaker whose electric vehicle brand, Luxeed, operates under Huawei's Harmony Intelligent Mobility Alliance (HIMA). Following Seres's September adjustment, HIMA plans to focus more resources on Luxeed and other brands, with Chery's Luxeed receiving increased attention.

SAIC Motor Corp. Ltd.

SAIC Motor Corp. Ltd. is one of Huawei’s Harmony Intelligent Mobility Alliance (HIMA) partners. Its Shangjie brand is among five car brands under HIMA, alongside Aito, Luxeed, Maextro, and Stelato.

Anhui Jianghuai Automobile Group Corp.

Anhui Jianghuai Automobile Group Corp. is one of the five automaker partners under Huawei's Harmony Intelligent Mobility Alliance (HIMA). Its brand under the alliance is Maextro. Following September adjustments, HIMA plans to focus more resources on Maextro and other non-Aito brands to ensure they receive sufficient support.

BAIC BluePark New Energy Technology Co. Ltd.

BAIC BluePark New Energy Technology Co. Ltd. is one of five car brands operating under Huawei's Harmony Intelligent Mobility Alliance (HIMA). Its brand is Stelato. Following Huawei's September resource allocation adjustment, HIMA will focus more on Stelato along with Luxeed, Shangjie, and Maextro to ensure these brands receive sufficient resources.
