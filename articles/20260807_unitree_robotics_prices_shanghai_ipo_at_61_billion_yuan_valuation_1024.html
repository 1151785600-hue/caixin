Unitree Robotics Prices Shanghai IPO at 61 Billion Yuan Valuation

Caixin Global | FULLTEXT | 445 words | 2026-08-07 02:28

Unitree Robotics

Unitree Robotics (杭州宇树科技有限公司) priced its Shanghai IPO at 150.8 yuan a share, valuing the firm at 61 billion yuan. AI firm DeepSeek is a strategic investor. Already profitable, Unitree reported 2025 net profit of 591 million yuan on revenue of 1.7 billion yuan. The listing process took a record 104 days.

Hangzhou DeepSeek Artificial Intelligence Basic Technology Research Co. Ltd.

According to the article, Hangzhou DeepSeek Artificial Intelligence Basic Technology Research Co. Ltd., founded by Liang Wenfeng, was a strategic investor in Unitree Robotics' IPO. It was allocated 933,399 shares (2.31% of the offering), subject to a three-year lockup.

China National Petroleum Corp.

According to the article, China National Petroleum Corp. (CNPC) was a strategic investor in Unitree Robotics' IPO, receiving a 2.23% stake allocation through its investment arm.

China Southern Power Grid

According to the article, China Southern Power Grid was a strategic investor in Unitree Robotics' Shanghai IPO. Its investment arm acquired a 2.23% stake in the offering.

China Telecom

China Telecom (中国电信集团有限公司) participated as a strategic investor in Unitree Robotics' IPO, taking a 2.23% stake. This investment highlights China Telecom's interest in embodied AI and reflects the trend of major Chinese companies backing robotics startups amid a competitive market.

Tencent Holdings Ltd.

A subsidiary of Tencent Holdings Ltd. served as a strategic investor in the Unitree Robotics IPO, taking a 2.23% stake in the deal alongside firms like DeepSeek and Chinese state-owned enterprises.

UBTech Robotics Corp. Ltd.

According to the article, UBTech Robotics Corp. Ltd. is a Hong Kong-listed rival to Unitree. It remained loss-making, with a gross margin of 37.7%, although it posted a comparable level of humanoid-robot revenue.

Shenzhen Dobot Co. Ltd.

Shenzhen Dobot Co. Ltd., a Hong Kong-listed rival of Unitree Robotics, remained loss-making according to the article, with a gross margin of 46.1%.

Deep Robotics

According to the article, Deep Robotics (深度机器人) is an A-share IPO hopeful in China's embodied AI sector currently facing regulatory inquiries, alongside other startups rushing to list before a predicted industry shakeout.

Leju Robotics

According to the article, Leju Robotics is an A-share IPO hopeful currently facing regulatory inquiries. It is among several Chinese embodied-AI startups rushing to list in a cautious market before what investors expect could be an industry shakeout.

Galaxea AI

Galaxea AI is the English name for the Chinese company 星动纪元. The article states it is an embodied-AI startup pursuing a Hong Kong listing, racing to raise capital before an expected shakeout.

AI² Robotics

According to the article, AI² Robotics is pursuing a Hong Kong listing as part of a broader group of companies rushing to go public before an expected industry shakeout. At least three such firms have already filed confidentially.
