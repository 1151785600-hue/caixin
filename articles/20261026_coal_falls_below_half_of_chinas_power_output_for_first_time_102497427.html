Coal Falls Below Half of China’s Power Output for First Time

Caixin Global | FULLTEXT | 566 words | 2026-10-26 08:33

1. China’s renewable-energy expansion has pushed coal-fired generation below half of the country’s electricity output for the first time: coal accounted for 49.7% of generation in the first half of 2026, and by the end of July installed solar capacity had surpassed coal power, making photovoltaics the largest installed source of capacity.

3. In 2025, China’s CO2 emissions per unit of GDP were 17.7% lower than in 2020; nonfossil sources accounted for 21.7% of energy consumption, up from 16%; combined wind and solar capacity reached 1.84 billion kW; and new-energy vehicles represented 50.8% of new vehicle sales.
