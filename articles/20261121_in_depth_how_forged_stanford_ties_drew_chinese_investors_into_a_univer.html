1. A U.S. federal court has ordered businessman Tony Hu, his mother, and Patten Holdings to pay $72.84 million to two Chinese investors.

The default judgment was entered by the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of California on Nov. 15, following the defendants' repeated failure to comply with evidence production orders.

The case illustrates how fabricated institutional endorsements and ineffective contractual safeguards drew $35.49 million into a cross-border deal for a little-known American university.

2. The investors, identified as Zhang and Chen, invested in July 2021 for a combined 39% stake in California's Patten University.

Hu's proposal promised a joint college with Stanford, a teaching center, and two laboratories in Shenzhen, along with exclusive student recruitment rights in China and the prospect of an initial public offering.
