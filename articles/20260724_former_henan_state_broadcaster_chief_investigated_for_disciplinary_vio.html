Former Henan State Broadcaster Chief Investigated for Disciplinary Violations

Caixin Global | FULLTEXT | 324 words | 2026-07-24 11:02

1985：

Wang Renhai began his career after graduating from Zhengzhou University with a degree in Chinese language and literature, working as an official in the personnel department of Henan’s provincial broadcasting and television bureau.

1986：

Wang Renhai became a reporter at the Luoyang bureau of Henan’s radio and television news center, before briefly working as a teacher at a training school in Xichuan County.

1987：

Wang Renhai joined Henan Television and spent two decades at the broadcaster in various roles.

2007：

Wang Renhai was appointed deputy director of Henan’s Radio, Film and Television Bureau.

2015：

Wang Renhai became a deputy head of the Henan provincial Communist Party publicity department.

2017：

Wang Renhai became editor in chief and deputy party secretary of Henan Radio and Television Station.

2018：

Wang Renhai returned to Henan Radio and Television Station, describing it at its 'lowest point.'

2019：

Wang Renhai was appointed the station’s president and party secretary, and also took top party and corporate positions at Henan Broadcasting Media Holding Group.

2022：

Wang Renhai stepped down as editor in chief.

July 2022：

Wang Renhai was included on the candidate list for the 17th Changjiang Taofen Award.

2023：

Wang Renhai became a member of the Henan provincial committee of the Chinese People’s Political Consultative Conference.

March 2024：

Wan Ke, former party secretary of Tianjin Radio and Television Station, was investigated.

April 2024：

Zhu Xiayan, a former president of Henan Radio and Television Station, came under investigation.

May 2024：

Zhang Huijian, former party secretary and president of Guangdong Radio and Television Station, was investigated.

October 2024：

Zhu Xiayan was expelled from the Communist Party and transferred to judicial authorities.

November 2024：

Wang Renhai received the Taofen category award of the 17th Changjiang Taofen Award.

May 2025：

Xu Longhe, a former deputy president of Shandong Radio and Television Station, was investigated.

April 2026：

Authorities announced investigations into Guo Zhong, former party secretary and president of Hubei Radio and Television Station, and Yang Jing, former party secretary and president of Heilongjiang Radio and Television Station.
