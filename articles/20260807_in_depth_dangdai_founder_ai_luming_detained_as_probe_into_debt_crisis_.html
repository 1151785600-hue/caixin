In Depth: Dangdai Founder Ai Luming Detained as Probe Into Debt Crisis Widens

Caixin Global | FULLTEXT | 567 words | 2026-08-07 02:28

Wuhan Dangdai Science & Technology Industries Group

Wuhan Dangdai was a major private conglomerate with assets exceeding 100 billion yuan before collapsing under heavy debt and questionable shadow banking practices (raising ~4.8 billion yuan). It entered bankruptcy reorganization in 2023 to address a roughly 70 billion yuan shortfall. State-owned China Merchants Group later injected over 10 billion yuan to restructure it and acquire its core pharmaceutical assets. Founder Ai Luming was recently detained.

China Merchants Group

According to the article, state-owned China Merchants Group injected over 10 billion yuan to help restructure Wuhan Dangdai Group and acquire its core pharmaceutical assets.

Wuhan Zhongbang Bank

Wuhan Zhongbang Bank, founded in 2017, was Hubei's first private bank. Regulators seized it on July 3, citing severe credit risks, making it China's first private bank taken over for this reason. Dangdai Group held a 20% stake as its second-largest shareholder. The largest shareholder, Zall Holding, had its founder investigated in June 2025.

Tianfeng Securities Co. Ltd.

Tianfeng Securities Co. Ltd., a brokerage formerly controlled by Dangdai, funneled about 9.8 billion yuan in financing to the conglomerate. Now under Hubei's Hongtai Group, it is under regulatory scrutiny and is a major creditor in the bankruptcy restructuring.

Humanwell Healthcare (Group) Co. Ltd.

Humanwell Healthcare (Group) Co. Ltd. was a listed core pharmaceutical asset controlled by the Dangdai conglomerate. It was acquired by state-owned China Merchants Group during Dangdai's bankruptcy restructuring to address the conglomerate's massive debt shortfall.

Wuhan DDMC Culture Co. Ltd.

Wuhan DDMC Culture Co. Ltd. was a listed company controlled by the Dangdai conglomerate. Over 3 billion yuan from illegal fundraising flowed to its off-balance-sheet affiliates. The probe includes examining suspected fund manipulation and embezzlement related to the company.

Wuhan Sante Cableway Group Co. Ltd.

According to the article, Wuhan Sante Cableway Group Co. Ltd. was one of six listed companies in which the Dangdai conglomerate held a controlling or major stake at its peak, when total group assets exceeded 100 billion yuan.

Changzhou NRB Corp.

According to the article, Changzhou NRB Corp. is listed among the six companies in which Ai Luming’s Dangdai Group held a major stake or control at its peak (assets over 100 billion yuan). No other information about Changzhou NRB Corp. is provided in the text.

Anhui Huamao Textile Co. Ltd.

According to the article, Anhui Huamao Textile Co. Ltd. was one of the six listed companies that Dangdai Group controlled or held major stakes in at the conglomerate's peak.

Wuhan Yangtze Crowdfunding Financial Exchange

Wuhan Yangtze Crowdfunding Financial Exchange is a local financial asset exchange. Dangdai affiliates became its largest shareholder, raising about 4.8 billion yuan by selling high-yield products to retail investors. The funds were funneled into the group's capital system. A full default in early 2022 triggered investor protests and a widening investigation.

Zall Holding Co. Ltd.

Zall Holding Co. Ltd., founded by Yan Zhi, is the largest shareholder of Wuhan Zhongbang Bank. According to the article, Yan Zhi was taken away for investigation in June 2025. Ai Luming may be asked to assist in matters related to Yan Zhi's case.

Hongtai Group

Hongtai Group, a Hubei state-owned entity, took control of Tianfeng Securities after Dangdai Group's defaults to isolate risks. It accepted Dangdai assets as collateral, lending funds back. Hongtai later became a major creditor in Dangdai's bankruptcy, holding claims exceeding 5 billion yuan, though relations later worsened over the risk assets.
