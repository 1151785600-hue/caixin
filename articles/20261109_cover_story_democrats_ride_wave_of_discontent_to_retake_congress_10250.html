Caixin Global | FULLTEXT | 638 words | 2026-11-09 10:57

Democrats captured both chambers of Congress in last week’s midterm elections in a backlash against President Donald Trump, winning Senate races in Texas, South Carolina, Kansas, and Iowa and taking control of the House. Texas Democratic victories for Senate and statewide offices ended a decades-long drought in statewide races.
