Caixin Explains: What China’s Proposed Shift to an Overnight Policy Rate Would Mean

Caixin Global | FULLTEXT | 619 words | 2026-10-21 03:07

1. The People's Bank of China (PBOC) is considering shifting its primary open-market operations from seven-day to overnight maturities, aiming to align its policy-rate framework more closely with the active money market.

[para.

1

]

On October 19, Governor Pan Gongsheng stated the bank would study this switch "at an appropriate time," and the rate on these operations would become the main policy rate, guiding the market-traded overnight repo rate, DR001.

[para.

2

]

2. The proposal is not an immediate change. On October 20, the PBOC announced overnight reverse repos—cash injections—for nine working days from October 21 to November 2, with daily operations capped at 1 trillion yuan ($149 billion).

[para.

3

]

This extended window addresses overlapping funding pressures from tax payments delayed by the National Day holiday and month-end liquidity needs, according to Wang Qing of Golden Credit Rating International.

[para.

4

]

3. The PBOC also plans to broaden loan-pricing benchmarks beyond the loan prime rate (LPR). Financially strong companies could negotiate with banks to use either DR001 or the LPR for floating-rate loans, while the LPR remains a benchmark for individuals and small businesses.

[para.

5

]

The one-year and five-year LPRs stand at 3% and 3.5% respectively, unchanged for 17 consecutive months as of October.

[para.

6

]

Additionally, Pan stated the PBOC would phase out legacy benchmark deposit and lending rates, unchanged since October 2015, replacing their remaining uses (e.g., judicial calculations) with market-based indicators, including average deposit rates published since the first quarter of 2026.

[para.

7

]

4. The rationale for the switch to overnight operations is alignment with market activity.

[para.

8

]

When regular reverse repos began in 2012, seven-day maturities suited banks' weekly liquidity management, and DR007 was more stable than DR001 at that time.

[para.

9

]

However, overnight transactions now account for about 90% of daily money-market trading, making DR001 a more immediate signal of short-term funding conditions, less influenced by longer-maturity risks, and it has become more stable with improved PBOC liquidity management.

[para.

10

]

Making overnight operations the main instrument would allow daily rather than weekly liquidity management, providing more precise control.

[para.

11

]

5. This change does not constitute an interest-rate cut.

[para.

12

]

Altering the maturity of the main policy instrument reforms how monetary policy is implemented, but does not, by itself, signal easing.

[para.

13

]

The overnight framework could support either easing or tightening, and the announcement does not establish a new, lower policy rate.

[para.

14

]

DR001 is determined by market transactions; the central bank's own overnight operating rate would act as the principal policy signal, steering DR001 toward the desired level.

[para.

15

]

6. Addressing why an LPR alternative is offered,

[para.

16

]

the LPR has become less representative of actual lending conditions, with average lending rates falling below the five-year LPR since 2023.

[para.

17

]

DR001 is based on transactions in a large, active market, and using it alongside the LPR could link some loan prices more directly to money-market conditions and improve policy transmission.

[para.

18

]

The proposal expands borrower choices without replacing the LPR wholesale or guaranteeing cheaper loans; financially strong companies would negotiate their benchmark, while the LPR continues serving individuals and small businesses.

[para.

19

]

7. Considering international practice,

[para.

20

]

this proposal follows the broad approach of central banks that guide overnight market rates.

[para.

21

]

The Federal Reserve targets the overnight federal funds rate, the Bank of Japan uses the uncollateralized overnight call rate, and the European Central Bank steers short-term rates through its overnight deposit facility rate.

[para.

22

]

China's proposed loan-benchmark changes echo the global shift toward transaction-based reference rates like the U.S. SOFR.

[para.

23

]

The aim is not to replicate another system wholesale but to establish a clearer short-term policy signal, broaden pricing benchmarks, and strengthen their connection to borrowing costs.

[para.
