BlackRock China Unit Wins QDII License for Overseas Investment

Caixin Global | FULLTEXT | 406 words | 2026-09-18 17:56

BlackRock Fund Management Co. Ltd.

BlackRock Fund Management Co. Ltd. has won QDII approval from China's securities regulator, becoming the fourth wholly foreign-owned mutual fund manager in China to gain such qualification. Its general manager is Yu Beihua. The firm operates a wealth-management JV with China Construction Bank and Temasek.

JPMorgan Asset Management

JPMorgan Asset Management is one of four wholly foreign-owned mutual fund managers in China to obtain Qualified Domestic Institutional Investor (QDII) qualifications, alongside BlackRock, Manulife, and Morgan Stanley. This approval allows it to invest domestic capital in overseas markets, reflecting its active participation in China's outbound investment landscape.

Manulife Fund Management

Manulife Fund Management is one of four wholly foreign-owned mutual fund managers in China to obtain Qualified Domestic Institutional Investor (QDII) qualifications, enabling outbound investment. It joins BlackRock, JPMorgan, and Morgan Stanley in this approved status for global diversification.

Morgan Stanley

According to the article, Morgan Stanley is one of four wholly foreign-owned mutual fund managers in China to obtain QDII (Qualified Domestic Institutional Investor) qualifications. It joins BlackRock, JPMorgan Asset Management, and Manulife Fund Management in receiving regulatory approval to invest domestic capital in overseas markets.

Neuberger Berman Fund Management (China) Co. Ltd.

Neuberger Berman Fund Management (China) Co. Ltd. is pursuing a QDII license; China's securities regulator accepted its application in July. Additionally, in May, Schroders Fund Management (China) transferred management of three mutual funds to Neuberger Berman.

China Construction Bank Corp.

China Construction Bank Corp. is a partner in a wealth-management joint venture with BlackRock and Temasek Holdings. The joint venture has issued 58 fixed-income QDII wealth-management products, as BlackRock expands its China presence in outbound investing.

Temasek Holdings

According to the article, Temasek Holdings is a partner in a wealth-management joint venture with BlackRock and China Construction Bank Corp. This joint venture has issued 58 fixed-income QDII wealth-management products, serving as part of BlackRock's expanded presence in China.

Schroders Fund Management (China) Co. Ltd.

According to the article, in May, Schroders Fund Management (China) Co. Ltd. transferred management of three mutual funds to Neuberger Berman. No other details about the company were provided.

Fidelity Fund Management (China) Co. Ltd.

Fidelity Fund Management (China) Co. Ltd. denied market rumors in August of preparing to leave China. However, the firm has faced substantial management turnover and declining assets over the past two years, according to the article.
