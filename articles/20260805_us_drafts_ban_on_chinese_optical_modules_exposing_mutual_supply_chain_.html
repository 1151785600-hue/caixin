U.S. Drafts Ban on Chinese Optical Modules, Exposing Mutual Supply Chain Risks

Caixin Global | FULLTEXT | 505 words | 2026-08-08 01:16

Coherent Corp.

Coherent Corp. shares rose over 10% after reports of a potential FCC ban on Chinese optical modules. The company maintains significant China operations, including its Asia-Pacific HQ in Fuzhou, R&D in Shanghai, and production facilities in Wuxi. Its Wuxi unit Finisar employed over 9,700 in 2025.

Lumentum Holdings Inc.

Lumentum Holdings Inc. shares rose over 9% in premarket trading after a Reuters report that the U.S. FCC is drafting a ban on Chinese-made optical modules, benefiting North American suppliers of AI data-center components.

Marvell Technology Inc.

According to the article, Marvell Technology Inc. is a U.S. optical-communications company. Its shares advanced more than 8% after a Reuters report said the U.S. FCC is drafting a ban on imports of Chinese-made optical modules and data-center components, a move that could disrupt AI infrastructure supply chains.

Zhongji Innolight Co. Ltd.

Zhongji Innolight Co. Ltd., a leading Chinese optical module maker, accounts for over 60% of the global high-bandwidth (800G+) market with Eoptolink. Highly export-reliant (over 90% of sales), 61.7% of Q1 2026 revenue came from the US. It shifted production to Thailand. Shares rose over 13% on CPO news, drawing 5.2 billion yuan in fund inflows.

Eoptolink Technology Inc. Ltd.

Eoptolink Technology Inc. Ltd., a leading Chinese optical-module maker, holds over 60% of the global high-bandwidth module market with Zhongji Innolight. Its stock surged 13% on CPO news. However, a proposed U.S. ban on Chinese imports could impact exports, which account for 96% of revenue. It has production in Thailand.

Suzhou TFC Optical Communication Co. Ltd.

According to the article, Suzhou TFC Optical Communication Co. Ltd. rose over 17% in share price and attracted over 2.8 billion yuan in net fund inflows. It derived 74.35% of its 2025 revenue from exports. TFC shifted capacity overseas, setting up production in Thailand in 2023 and completing second-phase lines in 2025.

LightCounting

According to the article, LightCounting reported that Chinese companies hold seven of the top 10 positions in the global optical-module market.

Morgan Stanley

According to the article, Morgan Stanley estimates that global shipments of AI optical modules are projected to reach 141 million units by 2027. This forecast was cited in the context of a Coherent/Finisar facility in Wuxi, which alone could represent over 7% of that future demand.

Applied Optoelectronics Inc.

Applied Optoelectronics Inc. has operations covering optical modules, optical chips, and testing and packaging hardware in Ningbo and Taipei. In 2022, it announced plans to sell its Chinese mainland assets to Yuhan Optoelectronic Technology, but the transaction was terminated in September 2023.

Yuhan Optoelectronic Technology (Shanghai) Co. Ltd.

Yuhan Optoelectronic Technology (Shanghai) Co. Ltd. (宇翰光电科技（上海）有限公司) was referenced in a 2022 plan to acquire Applied Optoelectronics Inc.'s Chinese mainland assets, but the transaction was terminated in September 2023.

Finisar Photonics Communication Technology (Wuxi) Co. Ltd.

Finisar Photonics Communication Technology (Wuxi) Co. Ltd., a wholly owned unit of Coherent, employed over 9,700 people in 2025. Based in Wuxi, Jiangsu, it planned a 1 billion yuan investment in 2026 to expand annual capacity to 10 million units of 800G and 1.6T optical transceivers, representing over 7% of projected global AI optical module demand.
