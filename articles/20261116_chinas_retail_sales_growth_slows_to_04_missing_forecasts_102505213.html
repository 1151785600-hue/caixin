China’s Retail Sales Growth Slows to 0.4%, Missing Forecasts

Caixin Global | FULLTEXT | 309 words | 2026-11-16 10:41

1. China's retail sales growth slowed to 0.4% year-on-year in October, missing market expectations, as car sales slumped and earlier purchases continued to dampen demand despite the launch of “Double 11” shopping promotions.

3. Car sales remained the largest drag on retail sales, falling 21.4% year-on-year in October. The decline has widened in recent months due to changes in vehicle trade-in policies and new-energy vehicle purchase-tax rules. Cars accounted for 7.8% of retail sales in January-October, down from 9.9% for all of 2025.

4. Excluding cars, retail sales rose 2.5% in October, also a slowdown from September. Goods sales and catering revenue both weakened, with restaurant spending potentially affected by a shorter October holiday period.
