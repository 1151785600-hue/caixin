Moonshot AI Launches More Powerful Kimi Model

Caixin Global | FULLTEXT | 523 words | 2026-10-30 12:12

Moonshot AI

Moonshot AI is a Beijing-based Chinese AI startup operating the Kimi chatbot. It launched Kimi K3.1 Pro, a post-trained model with ~3 trillion parameters and 1M token context, excelling in coding, financial research, 3D creation, and agent tasks. API prices were cut. Valued at $35B, Moonshot is expanding enterprise services and preparing for a Hong Kong listing.

Anthropic

Anthropic is a U.S. AI developer mentioned as a competitor to Chinese startups. Its models Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 scored 58 and 56 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, ranking above Moonshot's Kimi K3.1 Pro (55). Kimi scored above Anthropic's Fable 5.1. Since September, Anthropic released new models, with its frontier models also ranking higher on Code Arena.

Meta

The article mentions Meta in two contexts: its "Muse Wonder Full" model, which ranks above Moonshot's K3.1 Pro on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, though no public API is available for it. Meta also introduced new models since September, alongside Anthropic, OpenAI, Google and SpaceXAI, and its frontier models rank highly on coding benchmarks like Code Arena.

OpenAI

The article mentions OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra, which scored below Moonshot AI's Kimi K3.1 Pro on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. OpenAI is also cited among U.S. developers (with Anthropic, Google, Meta, SpaceXAI) that introduced new models since September, and its frontier models ranked above K3.1 Pro on Code Arena.

Google

According to the article, since September, Google introduced new models, among other U.S. developers. Additionally, Moonshot held talks with Microsoft, Amazon, and Google about hosting its K3 model on their cloud platforms. No further details about Google are provided.

SpaceXAI

SpaceXAI is mentioned as one of the U.S. developers that introduced new models since September, alongside Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, and Meta. No further details are provided in the article.

Zhipu AI

According to the article, Zhipu AI (智谱AI) makes the GLM-5.3 model. Its average cost per task is roughly in line with Moonshot's K3.1 Pro, which was $2.11 in Artificial Analysis testing. No other details about Zhipu AI are mentioned in the article.

Alibaba

According to the article, Alibaba is referenced as the developer of the Qwen 3.8-Max model. In Artificial Analysis testing, Moonshot AI's K3.1 Pro had an average cost per task of $2.11, which was below Alibaba's Qwen 3.8-Max, indicating Alibaba's model was more expensive on that measure.

Microsoft

The article mentions that Moonshot AI held talks with Microsoft, Amazon, and Google about hosting its K3 model on their cloud platforms. No further details about Microsoft are provided in the article.

Amazon

According to the article, Amazon held talks with Chinese AI startup Moonshot AI about hosting its K3 model on cloud platforms. Amazon has since made K3 available through Amazon Bedrock and its Kiro coding assistant, expanding access to Moonshot's models.

Artificial Analysis

According to the article, Artificial Analysis is a platform that evaluates AI models. It runs an Intelligence Index where Moonshot's K3.1 Pro scored 55, ranking fourth, trailing Anthropic's models and Meta's Muse Wonder Full. It also measures average cost per task ($2.11 for K3.1 Pro) and hosts benchmarks like Code Arena.
