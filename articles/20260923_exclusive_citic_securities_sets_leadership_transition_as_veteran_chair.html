Exclusive: Citic Securities Sets Leadership Transition as Veteran Chairman Retires

Caixin Global | FULLTEXT | 305 words | 2026-09-23 03:04

Citic Securities Co. Ltd.

Citic Securities Co. Ltd., China's largest brokerage by assets, appointed Zou Yingguang as Party chief, positioning him to succeed veteran chairman Zhang Youjun. Zhang retires after a decade-long tenure marked by post-2015 market recovery, aggressive expansion, and quadrupled assets. Zou, formerly a surgeon, joined in 2017 as fixed-income head and became general manager in November 2024.

Hainan Huayin International Trust and Investment Co.

Hainan Huayin International Trust and Investment Co. was an earlier workplace of Zou Yingguang, where he built his track record in fixed income before moving to Huaxia Securities and later senior roles at CSC Financial Co. Ltd.

Huaxia Securities

Zou Yingguang, now set to lead Citic Securities, built his fixed-income track record at Huaxia Securities before taking senior roles at CSC Financial. He joined Citic in 2017 to run its fixed-income operations.

CSC Financial Co. Ltd.

CSC Financial Co. Ltd. is a financial firm where Zou Yingguang held senior roles before joining Citic Securities. He briefly returned there in late 2023, shortly after its then-general manager Li Geping was detained by authorities.

Citic Group

Citic Group is the parent company of Citic Securities, China's largest brokerage by assets. Zou Yingguang returned to Citic Group in October 2024 before his formal appointment as the brokerage's general manager the following month. The group provides institutional continuity as leadership transitions occur.

Guangzhou Securities

Guangzhou Securities was acquired by Citic Securities under veteran chairman Zhang Youjun's decade-long stewardship. The acquisition, completed and integrated with Hong Kong-based CLSA, helped Citic Securities expand aggressively and grow its total assets more than fourfold.

CLSA

CLSA is a Hong Kong-based financial services firm that was integrated into Citic Securities under former chairman Zhang Youjun's leadership. This acquisition helped expand Citic Securities' global reach and strengthen its international operations.
