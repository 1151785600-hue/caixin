The best guest shifts in Hong Kong and Macau amid Asia’s 50 Best Bars 2026

SCMP | FREE DEEP | 1780 words | 2026-07-31

All of Hong Kong and Macau are gearing up for a week of mixology festivities as Asia’s 50 Best Bars looks to descend on our sister SAR for its second year, announcing this year’s list next Tuesday.

In the days before and after, both Macau and Hong Kong will be flush with the region’s top mixologists, either here to receive awards or soak in the cities – or to take over many of our city’s world-class bars. From mega shifts like at Argo or Wing Lei Bar to unique one-off shifts featuring bars from Mongolia, Nepal, Phnom Penh and, of course, Singapore, Bangkok and Seoul, here are the guest shifts this week you don’t want to miss.

Friday, July 24

Green Room x Three x Co

Ranked the No 15 best bar in Asia last year, Kuala Lumpur’s Three X Co is heading all the way up to the 49th floor of Upper House to take over Green Room for one night only. Represented this time by bar manager Rachel Hor and senior bartender Chris Ang, Three X Co focuses on contemporary cocktails such as those from its IndiVDuality menu. The Quiet Quinn riffs on the martini with a Tanqueray 10 base, a chrysanthemum infusion and Yzaguirre Dry Reserva Spanish vermouth, topped with orange bitters and grapefruit zest.

Where: Green Room, 49/F, Upper House Hong Kong, Admiralty

When: 6.30pm-9.30pm

The Local Pour – Dara x Flair Up

Dara, celebrated for its modern Filipino dishes and vibrant neighbourhood atmosphere, is teaming up with “flairtender” Washam Limbu for a special collaboration titled The Local Pour.

Limbu is the co-founder of Flair Up and frequently participates in the Young Chef, Young Waiter, Young Mixologist (YYY) competitions. He took home the 2026 YYY Story Teller Global title and was named the Young Mixologist of the Year in 2025, when he was also crowned champion in the 2025 Ichiko Cocktail Competition Hong Kong event.

Guests can expect high-energy performance, artisanal craft cocktails and a commitment to locally produced ingredients.

Where: Dara, Shop 3, Soho 189, 189 Queen’s Road West, Sai Ying Pun

When: 7pm until sold out

Saturday, July 25

The Road Less Travelled – Wing Lei Bar x Nomad Lab x Barc x Kiki Lounge

Even though Wynn Palace and Wing Lei Bar start their festivities earlier in the week, one of our favourites on the itinerary has to be this one – gathering bars from Mongolia, Nepal and the Isle of Man.

All three bars are represented by their co-founders. Nomad Lab’s Awaraa Awarzed is coming in from Ulaanbaatar as the self-proclaimed “first cocktail lab in Mongolia”, while Barc’s Abhishek Tuladhar is arriving from Nepal. The latter won the 2025 Michter’s Art of Hospitality award from Asia’s 50 Best Bars last year. Rounding out the takeover is Drew Fleming from Kiki Lounge in Isle of Man, the winner of the Spirits Business Awards Bar of the Year in 2024.

Where: Wing Lei Bar, West Esplanade, G/F, Wynn Palace, Avenida da Nave Desportiva, Coloane-Taipa, Macau

When: 5pm-8pm

25:00 x Thaipioka

Bartenders Hope Narawut and Supawit Thinpitayanurat, also known as Miguel, are arriving from Bangkok to take over 25:00 Twenty Fifth Hour, representing Thaipioka, which focuses on equal parts cocktails and music. Back home, Thaipioka offers fruit-centric cocktails like the Kyoho Balsamic Negroni, adding vinegar, shiso bitters and kyoho grapes to the traditional profile, or the Orange Thai Milk Tea, which soups up the citrus drink with rum, coffee, caramel and fresh orange.

Where: 13/F, Luk Yu Building, 24-26 Stanley Street, Central

When: 9pm-1am

Sunday, July 26 (early)

C108 celebrates its second anniversary with Sora Bar

C108’s second anniversary on Old Bailey Street is coming hot on the heels of its updated menu earlier in the month. Taking centre stage for Sunday’s anniversary party is a guest shift by Jonas Vittur, director of bars at Rosewood Phnom Penh’s rooftop bar, Sora, and its head bartender Phirun Lek.

Last year, the sky bar was ranked No 65 on Asia’s 50 Best Bars list and won the Ketel One Sustainable Bar Award. C108 is also working with Good Stuff to have four DJs play music all night long.

Where: 15 Old Bailey Street, Central

When: 7pm-10pm

The Old Man x AABBCC

Start your Sunday night at the top of the alphabet as AABBCC arrives from New Delhi to take over The Old Man on Aberdeen Street. The “retro-futuristic archive” bar sends co-founders Jairaj Singh Solanki and Aditi Singh, who are bringing cocktails such as a twist on the Godfather with dandelion, a Bellini using cacao butter, a caprese salad rendered in alcohol form, and a drink called the Mushroomisu.

Solanki’s former concept, Lair, also based in New Delhi, was ranked the 8th best bar in Asia last year.

Where: The Old Man, LG/F, 37 Aberdeen Street, Soho, Central

When: 6pm until sold out

Sunday, July 26 (late)

Dio x Bar Woody

There’s so much happening on Sunday and Monday that it’ll be easy to catch an early shift and a late shift on either night. Bar Woody has made a name for itself in Chengdu by anchoring its concept on the complexity of wood and herbs, with its signature cocktails such as the Cedar, the Benzoin and Sandalwood. Now there is a chance to try those earthy sips at Dio on Sunday, when Bar Woody’s Keith Lo and Austin Guo serve their wood-forward creations supported by Mizunara, featuring The Hachi Shochu and Wakshio 424 Gin.

Where: Dio, 8 Aberdeen Street, Central

When: 8pm till sold out

The Diplomat x Offtrack

Singapore’s Offtrack, last year’s No 23 best bar in Asia and known for its high-energy music and equally excellent focus on cocktails and food, arrives in Hong Kong to take over The Diplomat for one night only. At home, Offtrack’s co-founder Aslam Zainal and his team serve straightforward, inventive drinks like the Cora Middleton, combining raspberry, white almond, rum, lime, hibiscus and egg white. It makes perfect sense for Zainal to stage a guest stint at one of Hong Kong’s best bars for martinis, Irish coffees and burgers.

Where: The Diplomat, LG/F, H Code, 45 Pottinger Street, Central

When: 8pm till sold out

Monday, July 27 (early)

Quinary x Native x Milli x Chanel Adams

Hong Kong’s first home of molecular mixology, Quinary, is starting the party early on Monday with a triple-header featuring Singapore’s Native and Milli, alongside a special guest. Native is represented by co-founder Vijay Mudaliar and mixologist Amillie Chan, who are showcasing their unique approach to obscure and foraged ingredients, while Danny is bringing a menu of playful cocktails from Milli, a rooftop bar atop Singapore’s National Gallery. Rounding out the line-up is Chanel Adams, who most recently worked at the Mandarin Oriental Bangkok, and at Please Don’t Tell in Hong Kong before that.

Where: Quinary, 56-58 Hollywood Road, Central

When: 7pm-10pm

Tell Camellia x Gong-Gan

Gong-Gan, famed for its zero-waste philosophy, arrives from Seoul to take over tea-inspired cocktail joint Tell Camellia. Ranked No 74 on Asia’s 50 Best Bars this year, Gong-Gan is represented by co-founders Evan Noh and Willy Park. Their signature cocktails include the Si-Jang, featuring the premium Korean spirit Igangju alongside bourbon, lemon juice, roasted rice and banana beer.

Where: Tell Camellia, 45 Pottinger Street, Central

When: 8pm onwards

Monday, July 27 (late)

Kinsman x Bar Truce x Bar Notes

Wong Kar-wai-inspired Kinsman brings together Indonesia and South Korea in a double header featuring Bar Truce from Jakarta and Seoul’s Bar Notes. The former is grounded in Japanese mixology tradition with a twist, serving cocktails like the Ogura Dream as a clarified twist on the Alexander. Meanwhile, Bar Notes puts its focus on storytelling – its owner Ricky is also a freelance writer – serving concoctions like the Ssanghwa, inspired by the portmanteau “halmae-nial” – a combination of “halmae”, meaning granny, and “millennial”, referring to millennials enjoying foods normally associated with the elderly – containing rum, cream, egg and the eponymous bitter tea.

Where: Kinsman, 65 Peel Street, Central

When: Bar Truce: 8pm-10pm; Bar Notes: 10pm-12am

Socio x Modernhaus

One of Jakarta’s best – Modernhaus – arrives to take over Socio, Hong Kong’s main venue focused on upcycling community ingredients. Modernhaus was named the No 12 best bar in Asia last year and focuses on conceptual, minimalist cocktails. At home, they serve drinks like the Turmeric, which involves a turmeric gin, coconut chai milk, cream, salted tamarind and lemonade soda, and the Apple, which spotlights smoked manalagi apple brandy, koji, umami bitters and apple miso.

Where: Socio, 17 Staunton Street, Central

When: 8.30pm onwards

Tuesday, July 28

Campari x Wynn Palace Asia’s 50 Best Bars after-party

Tuesday is the day we’ve all been waiting for. After our region’s most excellent bars get their flowers, Wynn Palace is joining hands with Campari to host an all-out after-party in Macau. Responsible for the vibes are some of the region’s best. Look out for veterans of the list to host this get-together: Singapore’s Cat Bite Club (No 86, 2026); The Cocktail Club (No 12, 2024) and Carrots (No 34, 2025), both from Jakarta; Opium from Bangkok (No 43, 2025); and Bengaluru’s Soka (No 28, 2025).

Where: Palace Reserve Club, Wynn Palace, Avenida Da Nave Desportiva, Cotai, Macau

When: Post-ceremony till late

Wednesday, July 29

Argo Mega Friends Takeover

Argo plays host to an exceptional night of mixology as Argo Mega Friends returns with three new regional names. This collaboration brings together teams from three of Asia’s top-ranked bars. Bangkok’s Bar Us is represented by co-founders Aum Veerach and Taln Sudarat; Dry Wave Cocktail Studio sends founder Supawit “Palm” Muttarattana and bartender Ammie Suparada; and Taipei duo William Wu and Cloris Huang of The Public House round out the line-up.

Expect a vibrant atmosphere where these master mixologists serve up inventive, culinary-forward cocktails, such as Bar Us’ famous Jackfruit & Sticky Rice.

Where: Argo, Four Seasons Hotel Hong Kong, 8 Finance Street, Central

When: 6pm-9.30pm

Terrible Baby x Moebius Milano

Moebius Milano, ranked No 7 on the World’s 50 Best Bars last year, is housed inside a Milanese textile warehouse where Michelin-starred gastronomy, live jazz and avant-garde mixology collide.

For one night only, Moebius’ owner Lorenzo Querci and bartender Andrea Giraldo are taking over Eaton HK’s Terrible Baby. On the menu are cocktails like the Cocco & Salvia Negroni, which contains Bulldog gin and Del Professore Vermouth Rosso, as well as the aperitif Raspberry Lavorato Secco, which contains Campari and Rabarbaro Zucca.

Where: Terrible Baby, 4/F, Eaton Hong Kong, 380 Nathan Road, Kowloon

When: 7pm till sold out
