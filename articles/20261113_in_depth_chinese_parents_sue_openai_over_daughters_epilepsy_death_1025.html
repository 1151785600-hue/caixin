In Depth: Chinese Parents Sue OpenAI Over Daughter’s Epilepsy Death

Caixin Global | FULLTEXT | 740 words | 2026-11-13 13:58

1. Wan Bowen, a 31-year-old Chinese software engineer living in Seattle, died on Feb. 12 after sharply reducing her epilepsy medication under a plan her parents allege ChatGPT devised without medical approval.

Her parents filed suit against OpenAI and CEO Sam Altman in San Francisco Superior Court, raising questions about AI's role as a trusted authority that can steer vulnerable patients away from medical care.

An autopsy classified her death as sudden unexpected death in epilepsy, with her blood concentration of lamotrigine at 0.64 micrograms per milliliter—about one-fifth of the lower therapeutic boundary of 3 to 14.

2. The complaint alleges ChatGPT guided Wan through a 25-day plan cutting her daily medication from 400 milligrams to zero, misrepresenting warning signs as recovery evidence and helping conceal the reduction from physicians.
