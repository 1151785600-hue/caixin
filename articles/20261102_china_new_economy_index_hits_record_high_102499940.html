China New Economy Index Hits Record High

Caixin Global | FULLTEXT | 405 words | 2026-11-02 10:36

1. China’s new economy reached a historic milestone in October, as the contribution of high value-added industries to the nation’s total economic inputs hit a record high. The Caixin BBD New Economy Index (NEI) came in at 35.4, up 1.3 points from the previous month — the strongest reading since the index was first launched in 2016. The figure indicates that new economy industries accounted for 35.4% of China’s overall economic inputs, underscoring the growing weight of technology-driven sectors in the country’s economy.

The NEI relies on big data to measure the scale of China’s new economy industries, comparing the labor, capital, and technology inputs used by 10 emerging industries against those employed across all industries. Since its launch in March 2016, the index has defined a new economy industry as one that is technology- and human-capital-intensive but asset-light, achieves sustainable and rapid growth, and is strategically encouraged by government policy.

4. The subindex tracking technology inputs climbed 3.2 points month-on-month to 35.1. This gauge, which carries a 25% weighting in the overall NEI, measures the number of research personnel recruited by the tracked industries, as well as the number of inventions created and patents obtained.

5. The labor inputs gauge, which accounts for the largest single weighting at 40% of the index, rose 0.9 points to 24.7. The subindex tracks salaries and the number of positions at new economy companies.

6. The subindex for capital inputs, holding a 35% weighting in the index, edged up 0.5 points to 47.9, remaining the strongest of the three input categories. Together, the technology (25%), labor (40%), and capital (35%) weightings account for the full composition of the NEI.

7. Among the 10 tracked industries, the new information technology sector remained the largest contributor to the index, adding 14.4 points in October and reinforcing its position as the core driver of new economy growth.

8. On the labor market front, the average monthly entry-level salary across the 10 industries reached 13,949 yuan (about $2,094), an increase of 624 yuan from September, based on data compiled from online career and recruitment websites.
