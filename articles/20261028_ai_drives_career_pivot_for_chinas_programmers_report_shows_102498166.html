AI Drives Career Pivot for China’s Programmers, Report Shows

Caixin Global | FULLTEXT | 654 words | 2026-10-28 09:20

1. A new report from recruitment platform Liepin's Big Data Research Institute shows that more Chinese programmers are shifting into artificial intelligence, chips, and robotics as traditional coding jobs contract, highlighting how AI is reshaping rather than eliminating the country's software workforce.

[para.

1

]

2. The share of programmers changing career tracks rose to 26.4% in the first nine months of 2026, up 6.7 percentage points from the same period in 2024, with the number of career switchers increasing 39.4% over three years. At the same time, newly posted openings for conventional programming roles fell 8% from a year earlier, while software and algorithm jobs in eight newer technology fields rose 20.4%.

3. The findings are based on job-seeking histories on Liepin's platform, with the report defining career change broadly to include moves into AI, semiconductors, and adjacent roles such as testing, operations, product development, and project management.

[para.

]

4. Career transitions are most pronounced among younger workers. Programmers with less than one year of experience had a career-transition rate of 39.1%, while those with one to three years of experience had a rate of 31.3%, the latter group posting the largest three-year increase of 13.6 percentage points. Early-career workers have enough exposure to assess their jobs but haven't developed deep dependence on a particular path.

]

5. For programmers with more than a decade of experience, the transition is more often an upgrade in responsibilities than a fresh start, with a transition rate of 27.8%, with many moving into technical management, systems architecture, and project management.

[para.

7

]

6. Career shifts were especially active outside China's traditional internet-industry hubs. In the first nine months of 2026, transition rates reached 33.9% in Xi'an, 33.3% in Wuhan, and 33.1% in Chongqing, all higher than in Beijing, Shenzhen, and Hangzhou. Xi'an and Wuhan have recorded increases for three straight years.

[para.

8

]

7. AI was the fastest-growing destination for career changers, accounting for 6% of programmers' new destinations, ranking fourth, and growing 68.7% from a year earlier. Traditional destinations like operations, testing, data, product, and project-management jobs still accounted for 75.54% of transitions, while new-technology fields made up 14.74%, and moves between programming jobs accounted for 9.72%.

8. New-field transitions rose 47.8% from a year earlier, compared with 17.8% for traditional destinations. AI and large-language-model work represented 36.74% of transitions into new fields, making them the largest entry point. Other significant destinations included embedded systems, smart hardware, semiconductors, and intelligent vehicles.

9. Semiconductor and chip work accounted for 15.03% of new-field transitions, increasing 44.97% from a year earlier. AI-related derivative jobs grew 352.73% but accounted for only 5.73% of transitions, with the report cautioning that the increase comes from a small base and that the scale of such roles remains unclear.

10. Hiring data shows new-technology software and algorithm jobs have grown 57% over the past three years and now equal 79% of traditional programmer openings, up from 48.8% in 2024. General AI algorithms and engineering made up 46.8% of new-technology openings, while embodied-intelligence and robotics jobs grew 84.6%, semiconductor and integrated-circuit roles rose 47.8%, and new-energy positions increased 36.7%.

11. Openings in large models and generative AI rose 7.3%, while quantum and frontier-computing jobs surged 670% but represented only 0.3% of the total. Pay is also diverging: the average advertised annual salary for new-technology software and algorithm jobs was 456,000 yuan ($68,000) in the first nine months of 2026, 1.6 times the 293,000 yuan average for traditional programming jobs.

12. Semiconductor and integrated-circuit software roles offered the highest average annual salary at 555,000 yuan, above the 524,000 yuan for large-model and generative-AI jobs, while embodied-intelligence and robotics positions averaged 490,000 yuan. The data suggest elevated pay is driven by specialized technical capabilities and industry experience rather than AI hype alone.

13. For programmers, the transition increasingly means applying existing skills in new settings rather than abandoning coding altogether. Algorithm expertise can move into AI, low-level development into smart hardware, and systems skills into robotics and industrial engineering, according to the report.
