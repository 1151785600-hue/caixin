Hong Kong Launches AI-Agent Governance Project

Caixin Global | FULLTEXT | 451 words | 2026-11-02 19:11

Bank of China (Hong Kong) Ltd.

Bank of China (Hong Kong) Ltd. is a member of the Hong Kong Monetary Authority's 22-member expert group for Project SAGE. This group addresses governance, cybersecurity, and legal risks of AI agents in finance, with a focus on developing frameworks for autonomous transactions and customer-facing services.

Standard Chartered Bank (Hong Kong) Ltd.

Standard Chartered Bank (Hong Kong) Ltd. is a member of the 22-member expert group assembled by the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) for Project SAGE. This project aims to develop a governance framework for AI agents in Hong Kong's financial sector, addressing risks like unauthorized transactions and legal issues. The bank contributes alongside other financial and technology firms.
